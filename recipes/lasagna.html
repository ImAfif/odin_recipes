Wonderful Authentic Italian Lasagna

Enjoy this wonderful authentic Italian lasagna recipe. It is a classic dish that is sure to please everyone at the table. The layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, and creamy cheese make for a comforting and satisfying meal.

Ingredients:

  • Pasta sheets
  • Cheese
  • Optional: Beef, tomato sauce, and spices

Steps:

  1. Place the pasta sheets in a pot of water
  2. Place the cheese in the pot of water
  3. Optional: Place the beef, tomato sauce, and spices in the pot of water
  4. Turn on the stove to high and boil all the ingredients together
  5. Serve boiling hot and enjoy
